SLS_ROBUSTER v2.1.0
-------------------

Visual & UI Changes

Settings → Channels now uses compact chips (display name, token ✓/✗, Show in
Monitor) instead of full-width cards. Click a chip to edit that channel.
Playlist is a dropdown of that channel's YouTube playlists instead of a typed
playlist ID. Remove sits at the bottom of the edit form and asks for
confirmation first.

The Schedule tab date is clickable and opens a calendar so you can jump to
any day, not just step one day at a time.

Updated the app icon.

Download the zip from GitHub Releases, unzip, and drag SLS_ROBUSTER.app to
Applications.

SLS_ROBUSTER v1.6.1
-------------------

Visual & UI Changes

The Stream Info tab now shows Year 1 and Year 2 results side by side instead of
stacked vertically, so both channels are visible at a glance without scrolling.

The Apply Change button in the Title + Archive tab now shows exactly which
channels are targeted — for example, "Applying to 'SLS Year 1' and 'SLS Year 2'"
— and updates live as you check or uncheck channels. Shows "No channels
selected" if none are active.

The Activity Log now has a collapse toggle (▲/▼) in its header bar so you can
tuck it away mid-session without going into Settings.
